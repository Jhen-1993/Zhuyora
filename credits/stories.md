# 故事版本與來源 / Story versions and sources

2026-11-16。以下故事是中文教學改寫與英文翻譯，不是原作全文、現代劇本或族語教材。章節與語法安排為本網站設計。女媧結局台詞節錄《淮南子・覽冥訓》，其餘角色台詞為指定或原創改編，見 credits/adventure.md。

## 西遊記 / Journey to the West

分類：中國古典神魔小說 / Chinese classical fantasy novel

六章入門短篇，選述《西遊記》主要情節。

A six-chapter beginner retelling of selected events from Journey to the West.

- [西遊記原文](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/西遊記)

## 女媧補天 / Nüwa Repairs the Sky

分類：古老中國神話 / Ancient Chinese mythology

依《淮南子・覽冥訓》補天情節獨立改寫，聚焦修補天空與止水，省略傷害動物的細節。

An original learning retelling of the sky-repair episode in Huainanzi, focusing on repair and stopping floods; animal-harm details are omitted.

- [閱讀參考來源](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/淮南子/覽冥訓)

## 后羿射日 / Hou Yi and the Suns

分類：古老中國神話 / Ancient Chinese mythology

依《淮南子・本經訓》的十日與羿射日情節，採常見「射下九日、留下一日」教學版本；用后羿作熟悉的故事名稱。

Based on the ten suns and Yi episode in Huainanzi, using the familiar teaching version in which nine suns fall and one remains. Hou Yi is the familiar story name.

- [閱讀參考來源](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/淮南子/本經訓)
- [香港浸會大學：射日故事介紹](https://bcc.lib.hkbu.edu.hk/artcollection/bp163/)

## 封神演義：哪吒篇 / Investiture of the Gods: Nezha

分類：中國古典神魔小說 / Chinese classical fantasy novel

取材自《封神演義》第十二至十四回，省略暴力細節。這是哪吒篇的短篇改寫，不是整部小說。

Based on chapters 12–14, with violent details omitted. This retells the Nezha episode, not the whole novel.

- [封神演義原文](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/封神演義/卷014)

## 嫦娥奔月 / Chang’e Flies to the Moon

分類：古老中國神話 / Ancient Chinese mythology

採用逢蒙逼取仙藥、嫦娥為保護仙藥而奔月的傳說版本。此情節與《淮南子》簡短記述不同；結尾思念為教學改寫。

This retelling uses the legend in which Feng Meng demands the elixir and Chang’e takes it to keep it from him. It differs from the brief Huainanzi account. The ending about longing is adapted for learners.

- [閱讀參考來源](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/淮南子/覽冥訓)
- [故宮博物院：嫦娥奔月傳說](https://www.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2020/05/21/u5ec5f51a9cf73.pdf)

## 百步蛇新娘 / The Hundred-Pacer’s Bride

分類：臺灣原住民族傳說 · 排灣族 / Indigenous Taiwanese oral tradition · Paiwan

參考史前館與臺中市文化局介紹的排灣族版本改寫；未指定部落。不是魯凱族巴冷公主版本，也不代表所有部落的說法。插圖是故事示意，非傳統服飾復原。

Adapted from Paiwan story introductions by the National Museum of Prehistory and Taichung Cultural Affairs Bureau; no specific community is identified. This is not the Rukai Balenge story or a universal version. The illustration is symbolic, not a reconstruction of traditional clothing.

- [史前館：百步蛇故事](https://icloud.nmp.gov.tw/Library/EPaperContent?a=212&id=203&nid=754)
- [臺中市文化局：故事介紹](https://www.culture.taichung.gov.tw/media/826082/文化臺中-no03-文化書摘.pdf)

## 鄒族：楓樹與天神足跡 / Tsou: The Tree and Divine Footprints

分類：臺灣原住民族神話 · 鄒族 / Indigenous Taiwanese mythology · Tsou

依原民會《原住民族文獻》與臺灣光華介紹改寫哈莫搖樹、天神足跡主幹。哈莫與妮芙努（亦譯尼弗努）的名稱、造人方式與事件安排隨版本而異，不拼接為唯一正史。圖像只畫樹葉、山與足跡，不臆造神祇、族服或祭儀。

An original learning retelling based on the Indigenous Peoples Council journal and Taiwan Panorama accounts of Hamo, the tree and divine footprints. Names, creation events and their order vary by version, including Nivunu. The artwork uses trees, mountains and footprints without inventing divine portraits, traditional clothing or rituals.

- [閱讀參考來源](https://ihc.cip.gov.tw/EJournal/EJournalCat/461)
- [臺灣光華／外交部：天神足跡之處](https://taiwanngo.tw/Post/86989)

## 噶瑪蘭公主 / Princess Kavalan

分類：臺灣地方傳說 · 宜蘭 / Taiwanese local legend · Yilan

參考故宮南院對蘭陽舞劇所據地方傳說的介紹，只取公主、海龜與地景轉化的主幹，另寫教學文字；不改編舞劇台詞。此來源不足以確認為噶瑪蘭族傳統神話，因此標為宜蘭地方傳說。

Based on the National Palace Museum’s introduction to the local legend underlying the Lanyang dance drama. Only the princess, turtle and landscape transformation are used; the learning text is original, not the drama’s script. The source does not establish this as a traditional Kavalan Indigenous myth, so it is labeled a Yilan local legend.

- [閱讀參考來源](https://south.npm.gov.tw/ActivitiesDetailC006110.aspx?Cond=fa4b280a-be38-406c-bc19-0ecf01056af1)
- [故宮：公主與龜山島的故事主幹](https://www.npm.gov.tw/News-Content.aspx?idstr=MDEwMDAwMDE%3D&l=1&p=10&sno=04013917)

## 封神演義：蘇妲己篇 / Investiture of the Gods: Su Daji

分類：中國古典神魔小說 / Chinese classical fantasy novel

依《封神演義》第四回蘇護帶女進朝、恩州驛狐妖侵身的小說情節，加入原創閱讀討論。明確區分蘇妲己與小說中的狐妖，不把小說對女性的描寫當成歷史事實。本篇不含原著酷刑細節。 收藏圖以前景小說妖姬與身後的將軍黑影，表現學術推論啟發的女將重塑，呈現同一人物的不同敘事。

Based on chapter 4: Su Hu brings his daughter to court and a fox spirit takes over her body at Enzhou. Original reading reflections are added. Su Daji and the fictional spirit are distinguished; the novel’s portrayal of women is not treated as history. Torture details are omitted. The collectible places the fictional enchantress before a scholarly-inspired commander silhouette, showing two different narratives about Daji.

- [閱讀參考來源](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/封神演義/卷004)
- [將軍妲己的研究背景](https://research.sinica.edu.tw/ancient-china-shang-woman-daji/)
